Veyra - Open-source resources and attribution

Project: https://github.com/beastgotfried/agent-jailbreak
Website: https://veyra-ibmz.vercel.app/
Date: 18 October 2026

This list covers direct software, data, models, and research resources.
It is not a complete list of transitive dependencies.
Public datasets do not all have unrestricted reuse rights.
The selected TF-IDF models use the project synthetic catalogue.
External datasets below do not fit the selected frozen model weights.
Archived comparisons and reference-only resources remain separate.

Research references

Cheap Canaries — Jun Wen Leong
Licence: Code: MIT. Data, figures, and documentation: CC-BY-4.0.
Use: Prior art for classifier/canary disagreement, KS, and conformal adaptation. Veyra does not
claim the upstream results.
Recorded revision: 90726d9d4d281373e6729a0319d50e9c32566c59
https://github.com/junwenleong/safety-classifier-shift-monitor
https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.11949

Failing Loudly: An Empirical Study of Methods for Detecting Dataset Shift (Rabanser, Günnemann, and Lipton; NeurIPS 2019)
Licence: Research citation; see the publication terms.
Use: Reference for dataset-shift diagnosis and the limits of detector interpretation.
https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.11953

Detecting and Correcting for Label Shift with Black Box Predictors (Lipton, Wang, and Smola; ICML 2018)
Licence: Research citation; see the publication terms.
Use: Reference for label-shift assumptions. Veyra does not implement BBSE in the selected
release.
https://arxiv.org/abs/1802.03916

SciPy ks_2samp reference (SciPy 1.13.1)
Licence: BSD-3-Clause software; documentation terms apply.
Use: Independent comparison for the implemented empirical KS statistic.
https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy-1.13.1/reference/generated/scipy.stats.ks_2samp.html

Scope and rights notes

The selected guardrail runtime does not use an upstream language-model checkpoint.
Whisper supports presentation captions only, not guardrail decisions.
Veyra does not implement scan martingales, MMD, or BBSE in the selected release.
Prior-art results belong to the cited authors, not Veyra.
ToxicChat has a noncommercial restriction.
Unverified dataset licences remain unverified.
Follow each resource licence before reuse or redistribution.
This attribution list does not establish commercial rights or IBM certification.

IBM LinuxONE, GitHub, Vercel, and Google Chrome also support the project.
They are hardware, hosted services, or proprietary products, not open-source software entries.
Microsoft PowerPoint and the supplied slide deck also require their own rights.
The video uses an original generated score, not a third-party music recording.

This document contains no raw requests, model weights, credentials, or private server addresses.
