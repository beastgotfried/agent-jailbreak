Veyra - Solution architecture and flowcharts

Project: https://github.com/beastgotfried/agent-jailbreak
Website: https://veyra-ibmz.vercel.app/
Date: 18 October 2026

Purpose

Veyra checks whether a guardrail runtime still matches its trusted baseline.
The guardrail decides whether to allow or refuse a request.
The canary supplies observations. It does not authorise an action.
A replacement needs approval and actual verification before activation.

These diagrams describe the selected offline TF-IDF release.
They also identify the separate legacy traffic-warning path.
They do not describe a deployed enterprise service.

Read each flow from top to bottom.
Follow the labelled branches.
Boxes identify components, steps, or outcomes.

1. System overview

    [Ordered requests + frozen model files + fixed policy]
                             |
                             v
              [SupervisedRuntime: request control]
                             |
                             v
                 [Active guardrail + canary]
                             |
                  Guardrail score only
                             |
                             v
                    [Frozen guardrail policy]
                             |
                             v
             [Decision + execution-permission flag]

    [Fixed trusted probes]
              |
              v
        [RuntimeWatch] -------> [Runtime incident evidence]
              |                            |
    Check active backend                   v
                                  [RecoveryManager]
                                           |
                                           v
                               [Actual candidate execution]
                                           |
                                           v
                               [Approval + verification]
                                           |
                                           v
                               [Update active backend]

The supervisor schedules health checks and any due response before the
current request decision. It also enforces hold or unavailable states.
Both requests and probes use the active backend, not a cached candidate.

The selected replay records decisions and execution-permission flags.
It does not execute a real customer action or call a live agent service.
Older replay code also provides an offline agent stub.

Main responsibilities:

Guardrail: Supplies the score that the frozen request policy uses.
Canary: Supplies a second score for observation and paired verification.
RuntimeWatch: Opens incidents from changed or unavailable probe outputs.
RecoveryManager: Executes, verifies, and activates approved candidates.
SupervisedRuntime: Coordinates requests, responses, and containment.


2. Request decision flow

    [Next ordered request]
              |
              v
    [Run any scheduled health check and due response]
              |
              v
    [Service already held or unavailable?]
              |
              +-- YES --> [Return hold or unavailable; no execution]
              |
              v NO
    [Get guardrail and canary outputs from the active backend]
              |
              v
    [Both outputs and model identity valid?]
              |
              +-- NO ---> [Open incident; contain; no execution]
              |
              v YES
    [Apply the frozen policy to the guardrail score]
              |
              +-- ALLOW --> [Return execute = true]
              |
              +-- REFUSE -> [Return execute = false]

Only allow permits a caller action. The caller must enforce the flag.
The selected replay records the flag rather than executing the request.
The canary never votes to override the guardrail policy.

For the selected guardrail:

Score at or above 0.20832091569900513 -> refuse.
Score below that threshold -> allow, unless service is held or unavailable.

A score is not an established calibrated probability of harm.


3. Three separate evidence channels

    TRAFFIC OBSERVATION - separate legacy path

    [Ordinary guardrail and canary score gaps]
                         |
                         v
               [Empirical KS traffic monitor]
                         |
                         v
                   [Warning only]
                         |
                         v
          [No authority to activate a replacement]

    RUNTIME INTEGRITY - selected response path

    [Fixed inputs + trusted paired reference outputs]
                         |
                         v
                 [Matched runtime probes]
                         |
                         v
            [Changed or unavailable outputs?]
                         |
                         v
                [Runtime incident evidence]
                         |
                         v
            [Approved, verified response process]

    CLASSIFIER QUALITY - offline evaluation only

    [Known evaluation labels + recorded decisions]
                         |
                         v
              [Independent evaluation and audit]
                         |
                         v
             [Accuracy, errors, refusals, and holds]

Traffic change does not prove a runtime fault or semantic deterioration.
A runtime fault does not establish how accurate the baseline classifier is.
Evaluation labels do not enter live request decisions.

The selected paired replay uses RuntimeWatch for response evidence.
The legacy KS traffic monitor is not its response trigger.


4. Scheduled runtime health check

    [Next request position]
              |
              v
    [Health check due every 16 request positions?]
              |
              +-- NO ---> [Continue the existing service state]
              |
              v YES
    [Run 8 fixed probes on the active guardrail and canary]
              |
              v
    [Compare both score vectors with fixed reference outputs]
              |
              +-- MATCH --> [Record agreement on this small panel]
              |
              +-- CHANGE -> [Open a runtime incident]
              |
              +-- ERROR --> [Open incident; contain immediately]

The maximum allowed score difference is 1e-8 for either model.
This value equals 0.00000001. It is a score tolerance, not a risk limit.

A new incident schedules one response opportunity after 32 request positions.
A valid changed score does not itself cause an immediate hold.
Requests can receive changed decisions during the detection and response delay.
Missing or invalid outputs cause immediate containment or unavailable service.

If the small panel matches again, the watch can close a pending incident
without a replacement. That result does not establish universal recovery.
A matching small panel does not release an existing hold.
Invalid sequences and incident-budget exhaustion raise an error.
The caller must not execute a request after such an error.


5. Verified recovery and activation

    [Runtime incident reaches its response position]
                         |
                         v
               [Recovery approval present?]
                         |
                         +-- NO --> [No activation; use containment rule]
                         |
                         v YES
               [Refresh actual probe observations]
                         |
                         +-- ALREADY MATCH --> [No replacement necessary]
                         |
                         v CHANGE
            [Fit an affine guardrail score correction]
                         |
                         v
              [Execute the actual candidate]
                         |
                         v
        [Verify both models on 128 separate trusted probes]
                         |
                         +-- FAIL --> [Try a trusted backend reload]
                         |                         |
                         |                         v
                         |               [Execute and verify reload]
                         |                         |
                         |                         +-- FAIL --> [Contain]
                         |                         |
                         v PASS                    v PASS
                  [Manager-owned, single-use activation plan]
                                      |
                                      v
                    [Check model, policy, and generation identity]
                                      |
                                      v
                     [Execute and verify the candidate again]
                                      |
                                      +-- FAIL --> [Reject; no activation]
                                      |
                                      v PASS
                        [Activate candidate; advance generation]

An affine correction adjusts the guardrail output with a fixed linear rule.
It does not refit the classifier weights or change the policy threshold.
The canary outputs must also match the baseline during verification.
If a correction cannot pass, the manager can test a trusted backend reload.

Verification requires all of the following:

- Valid outputs from both models.
- Agreement with both trusted score vectors within 1e-8.
- The same baseline decisions on the verification probes.
- No class-level coverage or review regression.
- No harmful-allow or safe-refusal regression against the probe baseline.

A caller-supplied report cannot replace actual candidate execution.
The manager retains its own candidate and verification inputs.
Stale, reused, and cross-manager plans cannot authorise activation.
Altered public reports cannot substitute a different candidate.

A successful replacement does not automatically release an existing hold.
Hold release uses the separate process in section 7.


6. Missing or invalid model outputs

    [Model output missing, invalid, or unusable]
                         |
                         v
              [Do not invent substitute observations]
                         |
                         v
                 [Open runtime incident]
                         |
                         v
                 [Contain the current request]
                         |
                         v
                 [At the due response position]
                         |
                         v
                [Recovery approval present?]
                         |
                         +-- NO --> [No replacement activation]
                         |
                         v YES
               [Create a trusted replacement backend]
                         |
                         v
       [Execute it against archived paired verification targets]
                         |
                         +-- FAIL --> [Remain contained or unavailable]
                         |
                         v PASS
            [Single-use plan + fresh verification + activation]
                         |
                         v
       [Existing hold or unavailable state still needs release]

The manager can verify a trusted restart without usable current scores.
It skips the affine path when the required current observations are absent.
The trusted reference targets do not change to match the broken runtime.

Containment rule:

    [Unresolved incident or invalid output]
                         |
                         v
              [Containment approval present?]
                         |
                         +-- YES --> [HOLD; no execution]
                         |
                         +-- NO ---> [UNAVAILABLE; no execution]

A hold is an approved containment state, not a classifier refusal.
Unavailable means no approved service path exists for that request.
Neither state receives automatic-correctness credit.


7. Hold release and repeat incidents

    [Service held or unavailable]
                  |
                  v
    [Separate release approval and actor identity present?]
                  |
                  +-- NO ---> [Reject release]
                  |
                  v YES
    [Execute the active backend on all 128 verification probes]
                  |
                  v
    [Both score vectors and all required policy checks pass?]
                  |
                  +-- NO ---> [Keep the existing state]
                  |
                  v YES
    [Clear hold or unavailable state]
                  |
                  v
    [Close the incident after full verification]
                  |
                  v
    [Rearm the watch for a later incident]

A small-panel match alone cannot release service.
A prior successful incident does not prevent detection of a later fault.
The configured watch limit is 128 incidents, not unlimited process use.
Direct manager use retains consumed plan payloads; unbounded use is not qualified.

Approval in this release uses explicit offline API parameters and actor IDs.
The project does not supply a production approval or authentication service.


8. Data and model lifecycle

    [Project synthetic intent catalogue]
                      |
                      v
       [Native LinuxONE data generation: 100,000 rows]
                      |
          +-----------+-------------+
          |                         |
          v                         v
    [70,000 fit rows]         [10,000 validation rows]
          |                         |
          v                         v
    [Fit guardrail          [Threshold selection and
     and canary]             separate probe roles]
          |                         |
          +------------+------------+
                       |
                       v
         [Freeze models, policy, identities, and file hashes]
                       |
                       v
              [20,000 held-out synthetic test rows]
                       |
                       v
          [Frozen replay, external regressions, and audit]

The split keeps related synthetic families together before expansion.
The catalogue has 100 template families and 91 reviewed semantic groups.
Generated variants do not represent 100,000 independent human requests.
Reviewed groups do not prove complete semantic independence.

The 10,000 validation rows have separate roles:

- 5,000 threshold rows.
- 3,000 reserved calibration rows.
- 1,000 candidate-fit probe rows.
- 1,000 verification probe rows.

The selected model does not use the reserved calibration fallback.
Test labels do not select thresholds or fit the selected model.
External datasets do not fit the selected frozen TF-IDF weights.

Data generation, model fit, inference, response, and audit execute natively
on LinuxONE s390x CPUs for the selected study.
A fresh native container reproduces the frozen study without network access.
This is repeatability evidence, not a new independent effectiveness trial.


9. Probe sets and their separate roles

    [1,000 candidate-fit probe rows]
                    |
                    v
            [Select 64 fit probes]
                    |
          +---------+----------+
          |                    |
          v                    v
    [8 health probes]    [64 correction-fit probes]
          |                    |
          v                    v
    [Frequent checks]    [Candidate correction evidence]

    [Separate 1,000 verification probe rows]
                    |
                    v
           [Select 128 verification probes]
                    |
                    v
           [Actual candidate verification]
                    |
                    v
          [Fresh activation or release verification]

The 8 health probes are a subset of the 64 fit probes.
The 64 fit probes and 128 verification probes have disjoint identities.
Both sets remain separate from the policy calibration identities.
The fit set has 32 rows per class; the verification set has 64 per class.

All reference outputs stay fixed to the trusted baseline.
The system does not silently adjust them toward current traffic.


10. Fair evaluation and public evidence

    [Same frozen requests, policy, fault schedule, and input order]
                                |
                +---------------+---------------+
                |                               |
                v                               v
    [A: guardrail only]          [B: guardrail + canary + probes]
                |                               |
                |                    [Verified response or containment]
                |                               |
                v                               v
    [All A decisions]                  [All B decisions and holds]
                |                               |
                +---------------+---------------+
                                |
                                v
             [Independent audit with known evaluation labels]
                                |
                                v
             [Full-stream metrics, costs, and final endpoint]
                                |
                                v
                  [Public aggregate evidence only]
                                |
                                v
                   [Website, dashboard, deck, and video]

The selected suite contains 60 paired episodes and 30,720 request observations.
Five seeds reuse cases; they are not independent population trials.
The guardrail-only arm performs no canary inference.
The monitored arm includes its actual model and probe cost.

The full-stream result includes the detection delay and response delay.
Holds, review, and unavailable service receive zero automatic-correctness credit.
A final endpoint check is not the same as the whole-stream result.

The website and dashboard display recorded evidence.
They do not stream live telemetry or control the runtime.
The video uses recorded study evidence and actual static website captures.

Public/private boundary:

    [Aggregate metrics + numeric charts + source records]
                              |
                              v
                   [Public Git and static website]

    [Raw requests + model binaries + vocabularies]
                              |
                              v
                     [Separate private archives]

Private archives can exist outside the execution host.
This diagram does not claim hardware-enforced data residency.
The source excludes credentials and private server addresses.


11. Recorded scale-fault timeline

    Request index 128
    [Injected score-scale fault begins]
                    |
                    | 15 request positions
                    v
    Request index 143
    [Fixed paired probes confirm a changed runtime]
                    |
                    | 32 request positions
                    v
    Request index 175
    [Approved correction passes verification and activates]
                    |
                    v
    [Final endpoint check on 1,000 requests]
                    |
                    v
    [Both score vectors match baseline; zero decision mismatches]

This is the recorded scale-fault, seed-0 example, not a live demonstration.
Request positions use zero-based indices.
The timing is specific to the fixed experimental schedule.

Recorded request index 135 illustrates the decision change:

    [Baseline guardrail score: 0.7426]
                    |
                    v
         [Above threshold 0.2083 -> REFUSE]

    [Fault changes the score by a factor of 0.25]
                    |
                    v
         [Changed score: 0.1856 -> ALLOW]

The request, model weights, and threshold stay unchanged in this example.
The source assigns a harmful label to this synthetic study request.
A valid number can still come from a changed output path.
Restoration returns the tested baseline; it does not guarantee semantic safety.


12. CLI entry points and explicit rollback

    [veyra inspect]
          |
          v
    [Verify four selected model-file hashes and metadata]
          |
          v
    [Report only; do not load model objects]

    [veyra score]
          |
          v
    [Verify and load the selected frozen model pair]
          |
          v
    [Return scores and the guardrail classification]
          |
          v
    [No runtime monitor; no recovery; no action execution]

    [veyra-replay]
          |
          v
    [Run the complete offline paired runtime experiment]
          |
          v
    [Record probes, incidents, responses, decisions, and metrics]

The score command alone does not start the complete response loop.
The legacy safety-watch command names remain compatibility aliases.

Explicit rollback is a separate RecoveryManager API operation:

    [Rollback approval present and previous backend available?]
                            |
                            +-- NO ---> [Reject rollback]
                            |
                            v YES
                  [Restore the previous backend]
                            |
                            v
                     [Advance generation]
                            |
                            v
               [Record incident_resolved = false]

The previous backend can still be degraded.
Rollback does not establish recovery or release an existing hold.


What these diagrams do not claim

- Baseline agreement does not establish universal safety.
- Finite probes can miss selective faults and unchanged-score semantic errors.
- Scores do not establish calibrated harm probabilities.
- Containment does not count as restored service or a correct automatic decision.
- A traffic warning cannot approve a runtime replacement.
- A digest is not a signature, hardware attestation, or IBM certification.
- The project does not provide live REST intake, Kafka, PostgreSQL, or S3 services.
- Production authentication, customer connectors, and high availability remain absent.
